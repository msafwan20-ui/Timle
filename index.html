Stop It — everyone gets the same hidden target time each day. You see the number, then it disappears the moment you start the clock. Tap again the instant you think you've hit it.

Guess It — tap to start, and a beep fires at a random point within the day's window. You don't know when. The moment it beeps, type your best estimate of how many seconds had passed.

Both games: one attempt per day, per game. Your error in milliseconds is your score. New challenges unlock at midnight, your time.
