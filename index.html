How to play Timle
Two daily games. Three rounds each. Trust your internal clock.

⏱️ Stop It

You're shown a target time. Tap once to start a hidden clock — no numbers, no progress bar — then tap again the instant you think you've hit it.

Each round gets harder: Round 1 is a whole number (like 4), Round 2 is a half-second (like 6.5), Round 3 is full precision (like 7.76).

🔔 Guess It

Tap to start listening. A beep fires at a random moment somewhere in the round's window — then type how many seconds you think passed.

All three rounds are the same difficulty here — no ladder, just three fresh random moments.

⭐ Perfect

If your time rounds to the exact same hundredth as the target, that's a Perfect — the best possible result, shown in gold.

🔥 Streaks & rounds

Each game gives you one attempt per round, per day. Finish all 3 rounds in a game to keep that day's streak alive — miss a day and it resets.

🗂️ Archives

Missed a day? The Archives tab lets you replay any past puzzle — free for now, during beta.
